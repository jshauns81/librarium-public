# Librarium brand assets

The active artwork is the supplied **Librarium A** vector family, with separate
outlined lettering and dedicated small/micro icons.
[Its source record](librarium-a/README.md) owns checksums and integration details.
the maintainer supplied the family on 2026-10-25 after rejecting the earlier image/trace.
The current application uses those supplied paths and theme color roles.

## Preserved earlier artwork

`librarium-icon-original.png` is the maintainer's initial archivist emblem.
Preserved byte-for-byte; SHA-256:
`82b67ed71f73840b66ca85136bc8e32ddc0060354fe0e5799d53326422aec7e9`.
the maintainer identified its palette as Catppuccin Macchiato.

`frontend/src/assets/archivist.webp` is the earlier 256px quality-90 WebP
conversion (23,528 bytes). `frontend/src/assets/archivist.svg` is the abandoned
139-path automatic trace. Both remain historical assets and neither is loaded
by the current application. They are superseded by the supplied A geometry,
not references for further tracing or color selection.
