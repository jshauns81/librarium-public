## Direction contract

THESIS: Let books, authors and series lead a generous reading workspace; bring
exact evidence and durable catalogue reviews forward when the task calls for them.

OWN-WORLD: Reading Lamp's Literata, supplied artwork, semantic theme roles and
bounded folio selection support readable title-led folios. Preserve identity and
function while replacing the dense bibliography target. Reading Room supplies
atmosphere and reading comfort, not a literal reader or invented cover assets.

STORY: Search, follow an author or series, see recorded positions, open a work's
editions and evidence, run a metadata review, and return to the same Library
record or filtered page. Keep review history, progress, cancellation, refresh,
matching, integration entry points and diagnostic access usable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A calm masthead/navigation, clearly associated search and a
composed collection. Default Shelves and remembered List keep the same source
series order. Use admitted cover art where available and title-led entries
otherwise. Give titles, authors and series room to read. Keep truthful
status with its book. Suitable desktops open selected-work detail beside the
list; phones and short windows use focused detail with Back. Available synopsis
is visible above engineering controls, with a quieter review action.

FORM: User-directed readable folio refinement. No identity change or concept
roll. Recorded positions retain actual sequence information. `seed: none`.

FINISH: [UX standards](../../docs/ux-standards.md) owns hierarchy, truth labels,
disclosure, artwork admission and acceptance; [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) and
the sidecar record target tokens. Verify implementation and rendered journeys
before describing the target as shipped. This brief supplies no shipping raster.

Constraints: read-only catalogue/LF adapters, exact version plus digest, literal
search and no ownership inference. Historical matches retain their date;
incomplete checks cannot prove absence; catalogue-only is not missing. Technical
information is disclosed by task relevance, and actionable warnings stay visible
in context. Reviews write only Librarium's job state, never book files or Calibre.
