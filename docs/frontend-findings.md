## Coverage and acceptance

The baseline inventory contains dynamic templates: a book row, acknowledgement or
history action may have many instances. Each family needs normal, conditional,
disabled, error and recovery evidence; a source callback is not a click result.
Do not equate a passed build, detector scan or representative screenshot with
exhaustive acceptance.

The recorded matrix covers: Library/Catalogue/authors/aliases/series/editions,
comparison, incoming EPUB, validation, assessment, correction, output, delivery;
queued/running/completed/failed/cancelled, retries, reconnect, stale/conflicting
pins, reload and browser history. Unsafe actions use synthetic state. Writer and
account activation remain disabled on the installed target.

Verify desktop, intermediate, tablet, 320px/phone, enlarged text, reduced motion,
all shipped palettes plus both System modes, keyboard/focus and coarse targets.
Browser emulation does not establish physical-device or screen-reader audio proof.
Full-site Impeccable and Anti Slop approval is not inferred from these bounded receipts. Physical-device/audio, real-provider and conditional-variant limits remain explicit in the inventory. Exact-head hosted source/container checks and release/target gates are separate.

Opening a disclosure is not sufficient evidence. Read and assess its entire
revealed content, nested controls, comparison usability, copy, limits and recovery.
The user's explicit clarification requires control-by-control coverage across
every surface, not a representative tour or a few composition samples.
