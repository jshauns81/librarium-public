# Empty-navigation rebuild offers, 2026-10-31

[L063](../decisions.md#l063-validated-rebuilds-for-empty-navigation) makes empty
navigation correctable when inspection is otherwise sound and the source planner
proposes a non-empty replacement. Books without a proposal stay held with the
planner's reason. Actual output still needs non-empty navigation, EPUBCheck and
preservation; delivery remains source-derived and requires writer approval.
Inspection stays v10 and historical reports remain readable. No installed service
or library write is activated.
