# Collection experience

Mode: Operate. Audience: the maintainer browsing his personal Warhammer and Black Library
collection, following authors/series and opening engineering tools when useful.
The original collection experiment uses labelled synthetic records; the connected
Library and Catalogue have separate evidence contracts. This brief is current
direction, not a record that every design target has shipped.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A generous, title-led personal library invites browsing, then opens a
powerful work-specific evidence and review space without losing the user's place.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve Reading Lamp's supplied Librarium A emblem/lettering,
Literata, coherent theme palettes and bounded folio motion. Interpret the
Reading Room inspiration as literary atmosphere and visual richness. Its reader,
book imagery and claims are illustrative, not literal implementation scope.

STORY: Browse books, follow author or series, select a work, understand editions
and available library evidence, and request a review when needed. Keep browsing
while durable progress changes. Retain matching, history, search, refresh,
integrations and diagnostics as task-appropriate capabilities.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Books and bibliographic identity lead a readable workspace.
Shelves opens by default, with a remembered List option and identical source
series order. Prominent titles, authors and series have room to wrap; search,
truthful status and the common action remain easy to find. Generous reading
measure replaces the dense bibliography target. A selected work gains sufficient
detail space with its available synopsis above engineering controls and a quieter
review action. Admitted covers enrich shelves; absent art keeps a title-led
entry. Detail sits beside the collection on suitable desktops; constrained screens
use focused detail with a dependable return path.

FORM: User-directed composition correction within the established identity.
The original annotated-bibliography selection and seed `d2322ceb3f186173` record
the 2026-10-24 experiment's history, not a constraint on current density. The final
L024 direction supersedes fixed compact rows. No new concept roll or brand
replacement is required by this correction.

FINISH: [UX standards](../ux-standards.md) owns hierarchy, status, disclosure,
cover admission and acceptance. [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) and its sidecar
specify target tokens and treatments. Source checks and actual rendered review
must establish frontend alignment before claiming the new composition ships.

MOTION: Preserve the existing keyed 300ms folio selection transition, with no
polling replay, focus reset or task delay. Reduced motion removes the effect.

## Retained behavior and boundaries

The original synthetic experiment's 5,522 generated works and 24-row read bound
remain fixture/performance evidence, not the real collection's size or a visual
density requirement. Retain intent feedback, bounded reads, literal queries,
URL-preserved selection/filter/page, cancellation of obsolete reads, idempotent
commands, worker leases and durable progress/checkpoints. No retry or recovery
may claim success without the saved server result.

Desktop selection retains list focus and announces the chosen work. Focused
detail provides Back to the same list position. Evidence and comparison keep
exact source values, checked input fingerprints and scope. Fictional failures
remain labelled fixtures; historical results remain dated. Existing acceptance
budgets and measurements live in the first-slice research/handoff; do not turn
those historical observations into new measurements.

Keep catalogue, saved-library reads and application-owned jobs within their
contracts. Covers require an admitted read path. The scoped target can read optional
local originals through same-origin pinned-digest delivery and bounded batch lookup.
Unavailable art falls back to title-led folios; no shipping cover is fabricated.
No book-file access, Calibre write, source-service change or production release
is authorized by this design correction.
