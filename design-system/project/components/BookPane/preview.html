About this book

Under the Warmaster Horus, the Great Crusade carries the Imperium from star to star. Garviel Loken of the Luna Wolves fights at his side and begins to see the first cracks in the brotherhood: rivalries among the Legions, a remembrancer who records more than she is asked to, and a moon where the enemy speaks of gods. The war is being won. What it is costing is harder to see.
